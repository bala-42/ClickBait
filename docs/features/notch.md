# Notch panel

The island: rhythm, layout, bean, home, ask, shelf, settings, music, volume HUD. Part of the project rules (see `CLAUDE.md`).

#### Notch panel (2026-10-14; new look since 2026-10-20)
**The island look (user's choice, 2026-10-20), ClickBait's colours.** The open island, its header, cards and player are adapted from MIT-licensed code (credited in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`): flush with the top, no ears (`NotchPanel.ear` 0); open 640 × 160 (8 top, 34 pt header, 98 pt cards, 10 bottom; the shelf 640 × 176), corners 22 open and 14 for the small island (wings, HUD, hover); opening and tab changes spring (0.5, 0.72), closing eases out in 0.34 s (`closeCurve`). Rhythm (`NotchPanel.next`, user's rule 2026-10-21): the pointer on the notch slides it out — the peek at once (`.peek`: `peekWidth` 160 wider, the bean small on its left, music bars right), open after `openDwell` 0.2 s still on it (a click opens at once); the pointer away for `leaveHold` 0.4 s folds it right back into the notch, as do Esc, ✕ and a push up. Typing in it (the ask field, the shelf's note: `Model.editing`) or Settings keeps it open (`Model.holdsOpen`). The home card's status line reads the model's health each time the panel opens (`OllamaProvider.health`: ready / asleep / not installed / Ollama off; the menu shows the same as a dot line each time it opens) and says so instead of "ready" when it isn't. The Ask tab sends to the chat at once, shows "Sent to the chat…" for `AskCard.sentHold` 0.7 s, then folds. Colours: one palette, `NotchPalette` (greys, `highlight`, `track`, `music`); states are `CompanionTone`; problems are Wine everywhere (`CompanionTone.problem`, #9B1B48: the bean's error glow and the companion's problem pills). The pointer is read from mouse-move monitors (`watchPointer`), not SwiftUI hover: a non-activating panel gets no hover events while another app is in front. Header (`OpenNotch`): icon tabs in capsules left (Home, Ask `bubble.left.fill`, Shelf `plus`), settings, sounds (`settings.soundCues`) and close right. Cards (`notchCard`): #141518, corner 20, a hairline, an optional wash of colour from below. Home: the focus card, 322 wide (on its left the track's cover, 66 pt, with the bean small on its corner; with no cover the bean large, via `BeanPlace`; from 108 pt a dot, title and source, then the track's position and shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat — `MusicBridge.setShuffling/setRepeating` — or ClickBait's keys and Open Music/Spotify; ↗ opens the player) and a 2 × 2 grid of pills with small beans (Ask, Shelf, Mirror, the player). Ask: a field whose Return folds the notch and sends the question to ClickBait's chat (`Model.ask`, `startTypedChat`). Launch hello (`greet`, `Model.greeting`): 1.5 s after launch the island opens on a sparkle card with the bean big in the middle (78 pt, `.hello`: pearl, happy eyes, a grin) for `greetTime` 2.6 s, then folds to the peek. It is the only launch hello while the notch is on (`Greeter.start(helloAtLaunch:)` skips the cursor's). When the companion sets off for a target (guide, tour, walkthrough step), the notch folds right down (`NotchController.makeRoom`, via `OverlayController.onGuide`) so it never hides what is pointed at. The focus card washes in the mood's colour while something is going on (`FocusCard.wash`); pill beans are solid (`BeanFace.solid`). Shelf: Files, the AirDrop pad (drop or click: macOS's AirDrop window, `AirDropSender`, `NotchPanel.airDropItems`) and the note. Palette and controls in `NotchStyle.swift` (`NotchPalette`, `PlayerBar`, `PlayerProgress`, `PlayerVolume`). State colours stay `CompanionTone`. **The bean** (2026-10-21; `Bean` in Core, `NotchBean.swift`): ClickBait's own notch character, one view placed by state (`BeanPlace`: 20 pt on the peek, 66 on the home card, 44 beside the ask field, 78 for the hello, hidden elsewhere) so it springs between them — a pearl superellipse, rounder on top and flatter below, with blush, kaomoji eyes (a new random pair per mood change, `Bean.eyes`), a drawn mouth and a badge (··· thinking, ! error, ✓ happy, z sleepy). Moods come from the companion (`OverlayModel.beanMood`, set in `AppDelegate`); at rest (idle, asleep, or only feeling the Mac; `NotchBean.isResting`) it is plain pearl with a soft white glow (user's call, 2026-10-21); states tint it and glow `CompanionTone` (Pumpkin listening and thinking, Shamrock working and happy) or Wine #9B1B48 for errors (user's pick). It breathes, blinks, looks toward the pointer, hops on a mood change, wobbles on an error and dozes after `Bean.sleepAfter` idle; Reduce Motion keeps it still. While the companion is idle it feels the Mac (`Bean.mood(for:)`, `MacVitals`; read only while the bean is on screen, every 3 s): panicked at ≥ 90 % CPU (trembling eyes, sweat drop), flushed when `thermalState` is serious or critical, tired under 20 % battery and asleep under 10 % unless charging; pointing at it says why ("CPU 94 %"). These stay pearl. `CLICKBAIT_TEST_NOTCH_RENDER` also writes `notch-bean.png`. What follows describes the earlier GIF design; its sizes, ears and springs are superseded by the above.
**Earlier design (2026-10-14), superseded by the island look above for the open panel; its wings, visualizer and HUD measurements still hold. Drawn to the user's GIF** (~/Downloads/477708210-…gif, 601 frames, 16.9 s; measured frame by frame: its notch 248 px = 185 pt, so 1 GIF px = 0.746 pt). Clean-room: boring.notch's code and assets never read (GPL-3.0). Pure black from the notch, no border, ears flaring 6 pt into the menu bar (`NotchShape`). **States (`NotchPanel`, Core, tested):** closed = the notch; music playing = "live activity" wings, +40 pt each side, artwork 22 pt on the left wing and a 4-bar visualizer in the artwork's colour on the right (GIF 1.6 s: 248 → 356 px); open = 537 × 161 pt (719 × 216 px), bottom corners 18. **Activation:** see the rhythm above (peek, click, fold); also a two-finger pull down (`PullGesture`) or a file dragged on (opens the Shelf); Escape or a push up folds to the peek. Opening springs without overshoot (response 0.45, damping 0.86; GIF 5.5–6.0 s); closing bounces (0.4, 0.58; GIF 9.06–9.5 s: 558 → 367 → 410 → 382 → 373 → 370 px). The window grows at once and shrinks only once the closing spring settles (0.65 s), so it never covers more of the menu bar than the black. **Open layout:** header in the notch row — Home and Shelf icons left (white = selected), settings (opens ClickBait's Settings) and battery % with its green gauge and bolt right; Home: artwork 101 pt (corner 11) with the player's own app icon as a badge, title bold 13, artist 13 in the artwork's colour, a 4 pt progress bar (drag to seek; `TrackTime`) with its times, previous / play-pause / next centred under it; the week: month bold, year grey, six days with today third (`CalendarStrip`) in a blue circle on a dark column, then today's events or "No events today · Enjoy your free time!" (EventKit, asks only on "Show my events"). Shelf (not in the GIF): dropped files (session-only references; drag out, click to reveal) and the note. **Music (`MusicBridge`):** Music or Spotify from their change notifications (no polling) plus one Apple Events read when it opens or the track changes; position runs on by itself while playing; controls and seeking by Apple Events (asked once per player); never launches a player; Music's artwork from the Mac, Spotify's downloaded from its artwork link — menu "Spotify Covers": Always (default, as the GIF) or Only with Internet Mode 🌐 (`AppSettings.spotifyArtworkAlways`); wings tuck away 5 s after pausing. **Volume HUD** (not in the GIF, kept): the volume keys (`InputInterceptor`'s tap, NX_SYSDEFINED 0/1/7) set the volume through CoreAudio (`SystemVolume`) and show a speaker and a level bar in 96 pt wings for 1.5 s (`VolumeStep`: 16 steps, ⌥⇧ quarter steps); fail-open to macOS (off in the menu, no notch, open, or the device refuses); brightness is left to macOS (private API). **Removed for the GIF:** the dark Aqua cards, header toggles and gauges, the agent dot, the mirror tab (and its camera permission). **Layers (`WindowLayers`):** menu bar 24 < notch 25 < dock 26 < companion and peel flight. **No notch reported** (a full-screen app's desktop hides the menu bar: inset 0, no side areas, every process): the last notch seen per display and size is used (`notch.lastSeen.<display>.<w>x<h>`); re-read 1 s after launch and on every desktop switch; `constrainFrameRect` keeps it at the notch. Measured live 2026-10-14: 197 pt closed → 227 swollen → open 549 × 161 centred → back to 197; wings 277. Debug checks: `CLICKBAIT_TEST_NOTCH=1` (hover, open, close; `CLICKBAIT_TEST_NOTCH_RENDER=<folder>` renders open and wings with a fake track; `CLICKBAIT_TEST_NOTCH_HUD=1` one volume-up, put back).
