# Notch panel

The island: rhythm, layout, bean, home, ask, shelf, settings, music, volume HUD. Part of the project rules (see `CLAUDE.md`).

#### Notch panel (2026-10-14; new look since 2026-10-20)
**The island look (user's choice, 2026-10-20), ClickBait's colours.** The open island, its header, cards and player are adapted from MIT-licensed code (credited in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`): flush with the top, no ears (`NotchPanel.ear` 0); open 640 × 160 (8 top, 34 pt header, 98 pt cards, 10 bottom; the shelf 640 × 176), corners 22 open and 14 for the small island (wings, HUD, hover); opening and tab changes spring (0.5, 0.72), closing eases out in 0.34 s (`closeCurve`). Rhythm (`NotchPanel.next`, user's rule 2026-10-21): the pointer on the notch slides it out — the peek at once (`.peek`: `peekWidth` 160 wider, the bean small on its left, music bars right), open after `openDwell` 0.2 s still on it (a click opens at once); the pointer away for `leaveHold` 0.4 s folds it right back into the notch, as do Esc, ✕ and a push up. On and off have hysteresis (`NotchPanel.pointerOn`, adapted from Sentient OS): it slides out only once the pointer is on the black itself (brushing past does nothing) and counts it gone only `exitMargin` 4 pt outside; both reach `topOverhang` 2 pt above, so a pointer pushed against the top of the screen counts. Sliding out gives a light trackpad tick; the panel's Spaces behaviour is set again on every placement. Typing in it (the ask field, the shelf's note: `Model.editing`) or Settings keeps it open (`Model.holdsOpen`). The home card's status line reads ClickBait's health each time the panel opens and says so instead of "ready" when it isn't: the worst thing wrong right now (`HealthLadder`, Core; idea from Sentient OS), worst first — Ollama off, the model not installed, a permission refused (Screen Recording, Accessibility, Microphone; Microphone not yet asked doesn't count), the model asleep — else ready. The menu shows the same as a dot line each time it opens; a permission line there opens the Permissions window. The Ask tab sends to the chat at once, shows "Sent to the chat…" for `AskCard.sentHold` 0.7 s, then folds. Colours: one palette, `NotchPalette` (greys, `highlight`, `track`, `music`); states are `CompanionTone`; problems are Wine everywhere (`CompanionTone.problem`, #9B1B48: the bean's error glow and the companion's problem pills). The pointer is read from mouse-move monitors (`watchPointer`), not SwiftUI hover: a non-activating panel gets no hover events while another app is in front. Header (`OpenNotch`): icon tabs in capsules left (Home, Ask `bubble.left.fill`, Shelf `plus`), settings, sounds (`settings.soundCues`) and close right. Cards (`notchCard`): #141518, corner 20, a hairline, an optional wash of colour from below. Home: the focus card, 322 wide (on its left the track's cover, 66 pt, with the bean small on its corner; with no cover the bean large, via `BeanPlace`; from 108 pt a dot, title and source, then the track's position and shuffle, previous, play/pause, next, repeat — `MusicBridge.setShuffling/setRepeating` — or ClickBait's keys and Open Music/Spotify; ↗ opens the player) and a 2 × 2 grid of pills with small beans (Ask, Shelf, Mirror, the player). Ask: a field whose Return folds the notch and sends the question to ClickBait's chat (`Model.ask`, `startTypedChat`). Launch hello (`greet`, `Model.greeting`): 1.5 s after launch the island opens on a sparkle card with the bean big in the middle (78 pt, `.hello`: pearl, happy eyes, a grin) for `greetTime` 2.6 s, then folds to the peek. It is the only launch hello while the notch is on (`Greeter.start(helloAtLaunch:)` skips the cursor's). When the companion sets off for a target (guide, tour, walkthrough step), the notch folds right down (`NotchController.makeRoom`, via `OverlayController.onGuide`) so it never hides what is pointed at. The focus card washes in the mood's colour while something is going on (`FocusCard.wash`); pill beans are solid (`BeanFace.solid`). Shelf: Files, the AirDrop pad (drop or click: macOS's AirDrop window, `AirDropSender`, `NotchPanel.airDropItems`) and the note. Palette and controls in `NotchStyle.swift` (`NotchPalette`, `PlayerBar`, `PlayerProgress`, `PlayerVolume`). State colours stay `CompanionTone`. **The bean** (2026-10-21; `Bean` in Core, `NotchBean.swift`): ClickBait's own notch character, one view placed by state (`BeanPlace`: 20 pt on the peek, 66 on the home card, 44 beside the ask field, 78 for the hello, hidden elsewhere) so it springs between them — a pearl superellipse (`BeanShape`: exponent 2.7, as tall as 0.77 of its width; user's pick 2026-10-21), with blush, kaomoji eyes (a new random pair per mood change, `Bean.eyes`), a drawn mouth and a badge (··· thinking, ! error, ✓ happy, z sleepy). Moods come from the companion (`OverlayModel.beanMood`, set in `AppDelegate`); at rest (idle, asleep, or only feeling the Mac; `NotchBean.isResting`) it is plain pearl with a soft white glow (user's call, 2026-10-21); states tint it and glow `CompanionTone` (Pumpkin listening and thinking, Shamrock working and happy) or Wine #9B1B48 for errors (user's pick). It breathes, blinks, looks toward the pointer, hops on a mood change, wobbles on an error and dozes after `Bean.sleepAfter` idle; Reduce Motion keeps it still. While the companion is idle it feels the Mac (`Bean.mood(for:)`, `MacVitals`; read only while the bean is on screen, every 3 s): panicked at ≥ 90 % CPU (trembling eyes, sweat drop), flushed when `thermalState` is serious or critical, tired under 20 % battery and asleep under 10 % unless charging; pointing at it says why ("CPU 94 %"). These stay pearl. `CLICKBAIT_TEST_NOTCH_RENDER` also writes `notch-bean.png`. What follows describes the earlier GIF design; its sizes, ears and springs are superseded by the above.
**Earlier design (2026-10-14), superseded by the island look above for the open panel; its wings, visualizer and HUD measurements still hold. The window grows at once and shrinks only once the closing spring settles (0.65 s), so it never covers more of the menu bar than the black. **Open layout:** header in the notch row — Home and Shelf icons left (white = selected), settings (opens ClickBait's Settings) and battery % with its green gauge and bolt right; Home: artwork 101 pt (corner 11) with the player's own app icon as a badge, title bold 13, artist 13 in the artwork's colour, a 4 pt progress bar (drag to seek; `TrackTime`) with its times, previous / play-pause / next centred under it; the week: month bold, year grey, six days with today third (`CalendarStrip`) in a blue circle on a dark column, then today's events or "No events today · Enjoy your free time!" Shelf (not in the GIF): dropped files (session-only references; drag out, click to reveal) and the note. **Music (`MusicBridge`):** Music or Spotify from their change notifications (no polling) plus one Apple Events read when it opens or the track changes; position runs on by itself while playing; controls and seeking by Apple Events (asked once per player); never launches a player; Music's artwork from the Mac, Spotify's downloaded from its artwork link — menu "Spotify Covers": Always (default, as the GIF) or Only with Internet Mode 🌐 (`AppSettings.spotifyArtworkAlways`); wings tuck away 5 s after pausing. **Volume HUD** (not in the GIF, kept): the volume keys (`InputInterceptor`'s tap, NX_SYSDEFINED 0/1/7) set the volume through CoreAudio (`SystemVolume`) and show a speaker and a level bar in 96 pt wings for 1.5 s (`VolumeStep`: 16 steps, ⌥⇧ quarter steps); fail-open to macOS (off in the menu, no notch, open, or the device refuses); brightness is left to macOS (private API). **Removed for the GIF:** the dark Aqua cards, header toggles and gauges, the agent dot, the mirror tab (and its camera permission). **Layers (`WindowLayers`):** menu bar 24 < notch 25 < dock 26 < companion and peel flight. **No notch reported** (a full-screen app's desktop hides the menu bar: inset 0, no side areas, every process): the last notch seen per display and size is used (`notch.lastSeen.<display>.<w>x<h>`); re-read 1 s after launch and on every desktop switch; `constrainFrameRect` keeps it at the notch. #### Shelf drops, rename and the AirDrop card (2026-11-01)
**Drops from any app** (`ShelfDrop`): the whole notch (folded, peeking or open) and the shelf card take a drop of anything: a file reference (Finder, VS Code), a promised or carried file (Mail and Gmail-in-Chrome attachments, Outlook, Photos, images from web pages and Google Images, the screenshot thumbnail) written into `~/Library/Caches/ClickBait/Shelf` (emptied at launch), selected text as a `.txt`, and a bare web link, downloaded only with Internet Mode on (🌐), otherwise the pill says so. The companion's pill says "adding…", "added X to your shelf ✓" or why not. User report: only Finder files and Chrome downloads used to come in. **Rename** (user's ask): a tile's menu Rename…, or a double-click on its name; the file is renamed on disk in its own folder, a name without an extension keeps the old one, a taken name is refused; the field takes the keyboard at once (the notch is made key: a right-click doesn't). **AirDrop** (user's reports): the count shown is the count sent (`AirDropSender.sendable`: real files still on disk, each once); no "Sent" (macOS reports "shared" when its chooser closes, whether or not anyone was picked); a drop on the pad joins the selection, so sending is cumulative; the chooser opens inside ClickBait's own black card, 460 × 340, centred under the notch the pad belongs to, a 10 pt gap below the island, as a sheet of that card (a non-activating panel on the Space in front, full-screen apps included: activating ClickBait jumped to the desktop's Space). More dropped while it's up: the chooser closes and comes back in the same card with everything. The card has a ✕ badge and closes itself after 6 s with no visible chooser (after Cancel the sheet lingers invisible). Apple gives no public way to draw the device list ourselves.

**Paste onto the Shelf (2026-11-01, user's ask).** Besides dragging: the Shelf's **Paste** button, right-click ▸ **Paste**, or **⌘V** after clicking the Shelf (a click makes the notch the key window; it doesn't take focus by itself). The clipboard becomes the same items a drag carries (`ShelfDrop.clipboardItems`): files copied in Finder first (Finder also puts their names and icons there), else a copied image, else a web link (downloaded only with Internet Mode, as with drops), else text (saved as a .txt). ⌘V goes to the Shelf only while it's on screen and no text field there (quick note, rename) is being typed in.

**"take me there!" (2026-11-01, user's ask).** Clicking the cover (Home card, Music tab, or the bean standing in for a missing thumbnail) already opened the player or the video's tab, but nothing showed it could be clicked. Now, under the pointer (`TakeMeThere`), the cover lifts 5% (not with Reduce Motion), the pointer becomes a hand, and the companion's pill says "take me there!" (`OverlayController.showHoverHint`: only when the companion has nothing else to say; taken away on leaving, if still showing).
