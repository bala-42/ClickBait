# Background ClickBaits (the agent dock)

Peeling answers to the dock, tiles, done cards and progress lines. Part of the project rules (see `CLAUDE.md`).

#### Background ClickBaits (the agent dock)
Holding ⌃⌥ while a plain answer or a snip explanation is streaming (`AskCoordinator.peelableRunID`, set only once the answer's model request starts — transcribing, orders, sorting and walkthroughs are past) **peels** it off: the run (`AnswerRun`) switches to `.background(jobID)`, its task, conversation, snip memory and chat messages go to `BackgroundJobs` (≤3, memory only, newest first), speech stops and the same press is the next question. `askTask` is let go so Esc, dismissing or closing a chat can't cancel it; only its card's ✕ does. A 4th is refused with a "three's a crowd" pill. Background answers never speak, point, refine or tour. Jobs are answers, snips, walkthroughs and file jobs; no other actions. Colours by `CompanionTone`: answer Pumpkin, snip Strawberry, walkthrough Lavender, file job Shamrock.
**Lavender holds as Strawberry Red does (user's rule, 2026-10-14; `PeelRule.Hold`, `holdPeels`, Core, parity-tested rule by rule):** planning a walkthrough still under way 0.5 s after planning began goes to the dock as a Lavender tile (`BackgroundJob.Kind.walkthrough`, tone `.tutorial`), exactly as a snip worked out in Strawberry Red does — a fresh companion at the cursor, nothing pointed at and no app opened while held (the plan's "in another app" loop doesn't open it), a full dock keeps it in front; the plan finishes in the background and its card shows the steps with "show me!" (`PeelRule.resumeAction`), which brings the window back ("go there!") and starts the walkthrough (`resumeWalkthrough` → `beginTutorial`; only now may a step open an app). The snip's own auto-peel goes through the same rule. **A walkthrough in another app** ("…in Safari" while Safari isn't in front) used to put "show me in Safari!" in front and wait 8 s; it lapsed as a no and the fallback answer opened Safari and pointed at its menu bar (2026-10-17 log). Now it goes to the dock after the same 0.5 s without opening anything (`PendingWalkthrough.app`); "show me!" opens the app, plans there in front and starts. Only a full dock still asks in front. **Declined or lapsed walkthrough → words only:** the answer that follows opens no app and points at nothing (`answerInWordsOnly`, read once by `respond`). **Fast answers keep their companion (user's rule, 2026-10-14):** if the explanation is already complete when the 0.5 s comes, it doesn't peel — no flight to the dock and no new companion at the cursor (`PeelRule.autoPeels(_:answeredAlready:)`; logged "Auto-peel skipped: answered within…"). Before this a snip peeled until its tour started even when its words were in. **Voice → pointer (2026-10-14):** a spoken question naming no target takes the quick element-list pick only when it's a short locating question (≤ 20 words, `InstantAnswer.allowsUnnamedPick`), and the pick is kept only if the control shares a word with the question (`unnamedPick(_:fits:)`); else the screenshot answers. Measured: 92- and 245-character rambles that merely contained "where" were pointed at "Split pane" and "Terminal". Instant pointing still moves the whole companion to the target and back (the user chose to keep it). **Voice routing (2026-10-14, the user's rules):** an app's name in a question is just a word; ClickBait opens or switches to it before pointing only when asked — "in Safari", "on Notes", "open Messages", "switch to Chrome" — or for a Mac setting (`TutorialQuestion.locatingApp`; was any whole-word mention, so "my message to Claude" switched to the Claude app). Walkthroughs keep `taskApp` (they ask "show me!" first). An answer may point only for an explicit, short (≤ 20 words) where/which/show-me request (`InstantAnswer.allowsPointing`; was "where" anywhere, so spoken rambles pointed at the menu bar); a control only the reply names is pointed at only if the user's own words name it, no synonyms (`unnamedPick`); the caret is read only from the app in front when asked, never one switched to. New decisions log at debug level. **Pill text is centred (user's rule, 2026-10-14):** every pill — `TinyPill`, `GuidePill` (all styles, not only tours), the confirm pills, the dock card's pills and badge — has equal room above and below its text, by its ink — the first line's letter tops and the last line's letter bottoms, so wrapped text too (`View.inkCentred`, `InkCentring`, lines broken at `wrapped`'s width; a line box left lowercase 1–2 pt low). Not centred across: lines stay left-aligned (tour labels centred, as before). **Companion at a screen edge (user's reports, 2026-10-14):** it flips once and stays put — hysteresis: it flips when it would cross the edge and back only with 24 pt to spare (a cursor near the line flipped it every pixel). Not far: the triangle goes to the mirror spot, 35 pt left of / 25 pt above the hotspot (was the whole box past the cursor, `flipClearance`, gone). Not snappy: the side change glides 0.28 s on a smoothstep, no overshoot, frames only while it moves (`CompanionPlacement.side/topLeft/step`, `OverlayController.flipBlend`; it was a one-frame jump — the follow spring smooths the cursor, not the side); Reduce Motion: at once. **"Where do I type?" And a snap "inside an actionable element" larger than 640×360 pt no longer counts as confirmed (`Snapper.confirms`): Terminal's whole-window `AXTextArea` held every guess; such pointers get the close-up check. **Peeling on its own (user's rule, 2026-10-14):** a first ⌥⌘ explanation of highlighted text, or a snip, still working 0.5 s after its model request starts (`PeelRule.autoPeelDelay`, `autoPeels`; the timer starts only after the 0.3 s double-tap window is ruled out) peels itself exactly as ⌃⌥ would — the companion flies to the dock and the Azure companion takes over at the cursor. An explanation peels while it streams; a snip until its tour starts, and then skips the tour (a local model always takes longer, so in practice every highlight and snip goes to the dock). A done card's clicked suggestion (asked in its reopened chat, `AskCoordinator.nextTypedIsSuggestion`) peels the same way after 0.5 s (user's rule, 2026-10-14), "show me the parts" too. A snip answer finishing in the dock also places its tour there (`placeTour`, unless `isLabelled`) and keeps it (`SnipMemory.readyTour`); the card then leads with "show me the parts", which plays that tour at once with no model call (`playReadyTour`, after the `stillShows` check). Other spoken questions and typed follow-ups only peel with ⌃⌥; a full dock (3) keeps it in front, quietly (logged). A snip's done card leads with "show me the parts" when parts were named; it and "go there!" go back to where it was asked — that window, found among the app's windows by the title and frame it had then (`HomeWindow.best`, Core, tested: title counts most, frame breaks ties and finds a retitled window; `ScreenContext.windowFrame`, saved on the job when its run joins the tile), un-minimised, made main and raised (`AXInspector.raiseHomeWindow`), then the app brought to the front through accessibility (`AXInspector.bringToFront`, `kAXFrontmostAttribute`: `activate()` asked by ClickBait, never active itself, can be quietly refused; first try logged "raised its window" yet the user stayed put, 2026-10-14) and activated, which switches to its Space. Titles match without the browser suffix and what follows it — Chrome's window title is "Netflix – Audio playing - Google Chrome – balaji" (the profile after the browser, mixed dashes; read from Chrome 2026-10-14, the old strip of only a final " - Google Chrome" matched nothing) (`HomeWindow.titlesMatch`/`pageTitle`); tabs match when one name starts with the other, since Chrome adds and drops status words like " – Audio playing" (`HomeWindow.tabMatches`, ≥ 4 characters): stacked browser windows share a frame, and a frame-only match raised the wrong one (2026-10-14). When no window shows the title (its tab is no longer in front), the tab is found by its page title, selected and its window raised (`AXInspector.selectTab`). A snip's home is the real window under the middle of the circle, not the front app's focused one: the app from the window list front to back but confirmed by its accessibility windows (`AXInspector.appWithWindow(at:)`), the window, title and frame from accessibility (`AXInspector.window(at:pid:)`, front to back). Not the window list alone: Chrome keeps invisible full-screen and strip-shaped helper windows on top, so a snip's middle landed on one and "go there!" raised the wrong stacked window (2026-10-14). Each return is logged "Go there: its window, by title / its tab, selected / a window by frame only / app only…"; **Browsers (2026-10-14, the user's "the right tab and the right window"):** accessibility only lists a browser's windows on the current desktop (measured: of 9 Chrome windows it saw 1), so in Chrome and Safari the job also keeps the exact tab (`BrowserTab`, Core, tested; `BackgroundJob.homeTab`): read when the snip is made (the windows whose bounds hold the circle's middle, picked by the title accessibility saw there — 6 Chrome windows were stacked on one spot) or, for other questions, when the job joins its tile (the front window). **Look and motion: HeyClicky's agent dock (2026-10-11),** measured from its demo video at ~2.1 px a point (look and motion only; spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-07-background-clickbaits-design.md`; sizes and rules in `AgentDock`, Core, tested):
- **Tiles** (`AgentTile`, `DockTileView`): 33 pt dark rounded squares (corner 10, #26292A at 90%, a hairline), a glow in the job's colour, the job's 14 pt triangle upright inside, a 6 pt status dot 4.5 pt in from the top-right (Azure working, Aqua green done — the chat's `AquaDark.aquaGreen` #5BD25B, its "Copied ✓"; grey failed/stopped). ClickBait's own colours, not HeyClicky's; done is never Shamrock, which means a Mac action (user's rule, 2026-10-14). One column top-right of the menu-bar display: first tile 32 pt under the menu bar, 27 pt from the edge, 42 pt top to top, newest on top. Working spins (the companion's cached spin; Reduce Motion: a slow 2.8 s pulse), done pulses (1.4 s) until its card is opened, failed is dimmed. A pulse only fades a pre-blurred glow (`AgentTile.glow`). One small window per tile and per card (`CapsuleController`; a card's window is made the first time it opens — making them all up front cost ~0.2 s and ~100 ms stalls).
- **Follow up in the card (user's requests, 2026-10-14; `FollowUpPanel`, Core, tested; `FollowUpPanelView`, `VoiceButton`, `CapsuleController.followUp`):** one persistent mini-chat at the card's bottom (below "go there!", so nothing above moves) that always takes both typing and voice: the follow-ups so far (`FollowUpThread`, ≤150 pt, scrolled to the newest, selectable), then one row — field, voice, send, fold (that focus order). The card's "text" opens it with the field focused; its "voice" (and the row's) is press to record, press again to stop: while recording the button itself is the companion's waveform (`CardWaveform`, `WaveformMeter`, black on the light-blue button, following `AskCoordinator.cardMicrophoneLevel`), a spinner while starting or transcribing; the words are added to the field to edit, then sent — never unheard. Pressed while the mic starts it gives up; silence → "Didn't hear anything. Press voice to try again."; failures say why and the microphone is off by then. No send while recording ("Press voice to stop recording first.") or while the last answer streams ("Wait for this answer first."). The chevron or Escape folds it (the draft stays); a send keeps it open; it rides along in the running card; the card folding keeps it (only a recording is dropped). The card's window takes the keyboard only while it's open (`Panel.takesKeyboard`, still non-activating). Recording refuses while a question is in front; ⌃⌥ is ignored while it records. What's sent is asked in the dock (`askInBackground`: the job's flow through `respond` like a queued peel — its conversation and snip memory, text only, no pointing or tour; `BackgroundJob.reopen`). Bug fixed: the voice start wiring used `?? reason` on a nil-is-success result, so every start showed as failed while the microphone stayed on. Debug check, no model: `open --env CLICKBAIT_TEST_CARD_VOICE=1 …/ClickBait.app` (records ~1.5 s, nothing transcribed). Buttons: Aqua gel (`AquaButtonStyle`), light blue with black text and icons (user's explicit request, an exception to copying no HeyClicky colours).
- **Folding (user's request, 2026-10-14; `DockCollapse` + `ShakeDetector`, Core, tested; `CapsuleController`):** the open dock folds 4 s after the pointer was last over it (checked twice a second, only while open), or on any click outside its windows; never while a card is open, and never on idle while VoiceOver is on. The tiles fade out in 0.22 s sliding 10 pt toward the edge (fade only with Reduce Motion) and let clicks through at once; only a 4×22 pt pill stays, 3 pt from the screen's right edge, level with the top slot (a 20×38 pt window), Azure if something is working, else Aqua green if a result is unopened, else grey; faint (0.55) until hovered. It comes back on a click on the pill (the accessible way; VoiceOver: "Show background ClickBaits", "N waiting"), a shake over it (3 turns of ≥ 12 pt each within 0.7 s, inside a 90×70 pt band by the pill; pointer moves are watched only while folded), a new peel (it must catch the flight), or a tile opened from the menu. Tiles fade back from where they are in 0.18 s; hovering a tile shows its question. Nothing becomes key, so the front app keeps the keyboard. Rapid toggling: every fold or unfold bumps a generation, and a fade that ends after the next one began leaves its window alone; a tile still fading in from a peel stays hidden if the dock folds first.
- **Peel** (`OverlayController.peel`, `AgentDock.spawn`): the companion's copy turns the job's colour and grows to 2× (0.1 s), flies `FlightArc`'s curve/swell/easing in a fixed 0.55 s into the top slot, big until halfway then shrinking to 14 pt and turning upright; the new tile fades in during the first 30% while the older ones slide down a slot (`AgentDock.slide`); the real companion stays hidden while the copy grows, then pops in at the cursor (`companionPopDelay`). Core Animation keyframes sampled 40 times, nothing in SwiftUI per frame; a layer rotation turns anticlockwise on screen, so the clockwise angle is negated. Fallback (`AgentDock.peelMotion` → `.inPlace`, Core, tested): Reduce Motion, no slot, the slot on another display, or no layer to animate — no copy, the companion pops back at once (`freshCompanionDelay`), the tile fades in; the job, its card and its task are the same either way (logged "Peel shown in place"). The copy fades from `AgentDock.copyFadeStart` (94 %), when it is already over its tile. Acceptance tests for the whole handoff — timing from the 0.5 s auto-peel, what's visible when, the job as one card from peel to result, and the fallback — are in `PeelHandoffTests`.
- **Cards** (`CapsuleView` → `AgentRunningCard` / `AgentDoneCard` / `AgentFailedCard`): a click on a tile hangs its card 8 pt under it, right-aligned, 288 pt of dark glass (corner 12, hairline, glow in the job's colour), 14/15 pt padding; title row: the question UPPERCASE 10 pt semibold, a lowercase badge (running/done/failed) in the job's colour, ✕. Running: terminal icon, the sentence being written (`JobSummary.liveLine` over the spoken text, 12 pt grey, ≤2 lines), the progress bar; ✕ stops it. Done: the summary (13 pt semibold, ≤3 lines, `JobSummary`), **suggested next:** ≤2 pills (20 pt) from the local model the first time the card opens (`Prompt.nextSystem`, `NextSuggestions`, text only, 4 s cap; slow → none, asked again next time); a pill goes there and sends it as a typed follow-up; **follow up** [text] goes there with the cursor in the field, [voice] goes there and listens as with ⌃⌥ until the next click or 20 s (`listenUntilClick`); [later, pls] folds the card (the tile stays), [go there!] activates the home app and reopens the chat with the whole conversation; ✕ dismisses. A chat open at that moment becomes a done tile ("your chat about …"). Failed: the reason, ✕ removes it.
- **Result pill** (`Bubble.jobResult`, drawn by `TinyPill` like every other pill — corner 11, 12 pt semibold, the tone at 90%, same glow; user's rule, 2026-10-14: the result pill must match the pill system): when a job finishes, a headline (`JobSummary.headline`, ≤45 characters, plain text: the first sentence, cut at a natural break leaving 25+ characters or at a word; user's rule, 2026-10-20: few words on pills) shows once by the companion in the job's colour for 5 s — waiting while ClickBait is listening/thinking/speaking, pointing, touring, walking through, or showing other words; given up after 2 min.
- Streaming is coalesced: background jobs pass streamed words on at most every 0.1 s, only the changed job updates, tiles read only their status, and the menu's "Background ClickBaits" list rebuilds only for new/gone/finished jobs.

## Done cards and progress

**Done card (2026-10-20):** shows the whole answer (`AgentAnswerBody`: inline markdown, code with Copy, selectable), scrolling inside the card only past 260 pt; the summary is only a fallback. `ImageRenderer` can't draw the scrolling case, so dock renders use a short sample.

**Progress lines and sounds (C6, 2026-10-20).** A running tile says what it's doing until its answer starts writing (`JobStage`, `JobSummary.liveLine(from:stage:)`): "Reading your screen…" (asked with the screenshot), "Thinking…" (text only), "Looking it up on the web…" (Internet Mode lookup), "Planning the steps…" (Lavender walkthrough hold); set on the flow and carried onto the tile when it's peeled. `SoundCues`: macOS's own Glass when a background job is done (`BackgroundJobs.onDone`) and Pop when ClickBait needs a yes (`onConfirmRequest`), volume 0.35, at most one a second; menu Sounds turns both off (`settings.soundCues`, on by default). Foreground answers make no sound. Focus isn't detected (it needs a permission prompt).

**Keeping awake (2026-10-21, idea from Sentient OS).** While any job is working, `BackgroundJobs` holds a `ProcessInfo.beginActivity(.userInitiated)` activity: the Mac doesn't idle-sleep and ClickBait isn't napped, so a long answer finishes untouched; the screen can still dim and lock. It ends when the last job is done, failed or stopped. Check with `pmset -g assertions` (look for ClickBait).

**Morning cards (2026-10-21, idea from Sentient OS).** Settings → General → Morning suggestions (off by default; turning it on asks for Reminders and Calendars full access — either may be refused). Once a day, 20 s after the first launch or wake after 5 am (`MorningCards.isDue`, `settings.morningCardsShown`), `MorningBrief` reads incomplete reminders due by tonight, today's events (all-day ones without a time) and the 10 newest remembered facts, builds `MorningCards.brief`, and asks the local model text-only (`Prompt.morningSystem`, `{"cards"}` structured output, ≤90 tokens, 60 s). Up to three cards of ≤40 characters (`MorningCards.parse`) become a done "Good morning" card in the dock: its body is the brief, the cards are its suggestion pills (`AgentDoneCard` shows up to three); tapping one asks ClickBait that, like any follow-up. Nothing to go on → no card that day; no usable reply or a full dock → tried again on the next wake. Never leaves the Mac.


**Card follow-ups keep the document (2026-11-01).** A card made from highlighted text (a document's passage, code) keeps that text (`Entry.documentText`); a follow-up asked in the card gets it again as context (`highlightIsContext`), as it was asked with none and the screen has usually moved on.

**File jobs (2026-11-02, user: "make shamrock green file manipulation a backgroundable task … like the dock thing").** Every copy-making file order (`FileAction.makesCopies`: shrink, convert, zip, combine) goes straight to the dock as a Shamrock tile (`BackgroundJob.Kind.files`, `AskCoordinator.startFileJob`): the files are read at once from where they're chosen (shelf, the document in front, Finder), ClickBait says "On it, in the dock" and is free; nothing is brought forward when it finishes. The done pill is a file line (`DonePill.Task.files`); the card shows the result ("Converted 1 item to GIF"), has no suggestion chips, and its "show me!" reveals the new files in Finder and clears the tile (`Entry.madeFiles`). Files made from shelf files still go back on the shelf. A failure shows on the tile like any job. With the dock full (3), the order runs in the foreground as before. Trash, move and rename stay foreground (quick, and Finder-only). Debug: `org.clickbait.debug.order` with the order's text runs it as if typed.
